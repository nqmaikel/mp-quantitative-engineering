## Purpose

MP Quantitative Engineering is a consultancy website presenting custom software development, automation feasibility work and predictive planning services. It organizes the offering around business problems, technical diagnosis and functional prototypes.

The website gives visitors a structured way to understand the available services and the methodology behind them before considering a development project.

## Website scope

- Dedicated service and methodology pages.
- Custom software development and automation feasibility information.
- Predictive demand, purchasing and production planning service pages.
- Inventory optimization service information.
- An editorial section with article and feed structure.

## High-level workflow

Visitors can start with a business need, explore the relevant service and read about the proposed working method. Related service and editorial sections provide further context for understanding the offering.

## Stack

React and TypeScript, with vinext, Next.js, Vite and Tailwind CSS in the project stack.

## Development status

Website project bringing service information, methodology and editorial content into a coherent public presentation.
